That's ~105. A few design notes for wiring them into onboarding:

Tag every seed with the prompt it answered (already in the schema as the seed's prompt payload) — the category is high-value portrait signal on its own ("gravitates to function/focus music," "rich autobiographical attachment," "large negative space").
The "negative space" cluster is gold and underused by most apps: a hated/skipped song teaches the model as much as a loved one. Consider storing those as kind:'anti_seed' (or a sentiment:'negative' flag) so they repel rather than seed.
Rotate one-per-cluster, not random — otherwise you get five "memory" songs and a lopsided graph.
